# Decathlon Review — Honest Assessment Requested

## What I'm asking of you

Below are **two solo dice-only "decathlons"** — each a set of 10 short push-your-luck mini-games played in sequence with a single grand total as the final score. I want your honest read on how each event works and how each decathlon holds together.

I'm hiding attribution. Refer to them as **Decathlon A** and **Decathlon B** in your response.

You may recognize some events by name or mechanics. **Please set that aside.** Rate each event on its own merits. If at the very end you want to speculate about provenance, go ahead — but your ratings should be locked in before you do.

You do **not** need to play or simulate anything. Read the rules and reason.

---

## Design targets (the bar both are trying to clear)

1. **Simple but elegant.** Rules fit in a paragraph; mechanics interlock cleanly.
2. **Real decisions.** At key moments the player has a choice whose right answer isn't obvious — math fuzzy enough that even an experienced player has to think.
3. **Push-your-luck tension.** A "just one more roll" pull, with real cost to greed.
4. **Agency.** Player decisions measurably shift outcome; the game does not "play itself."
5. **Useful score variance.** Scores spread across a meaningful range; ceiling and floor are rare but possible.
6. **Decathlon coherence.** The 10 events feel distinct — different mental gears — not variations on one idea.

## Failure modes to watch for (name them when you see them)

- **Dominant strategy** — one choice is always best; "decision" is illusory.
- **Plays itself** — no meaningful input after setup; outcome is just dice.
- **Flat risk curve** — stopping early feels equivalent to pushing; no real tension.
- **Cliff-edge swinginess** — one die can erase a whole round's good decisions with no mitigation.
- **Decision paralysis / opaque math** — choices exist but are too hard to reason about; reduces to guessing.
- **Mechanical duplication** — too similar to another event in the same decathlon.
- **Dead weight** — low engagement, low variance, or pure arithmetic with no tension.

## Scoring rubric

For each event, rate on four axes, 1–5 (half-points allowed; 1 = poor, 3 = acceptable, 5 = exemplary):

- **Elegance** — how clean and quickly-graspable the rules are.
- **Agency** — how much player decisions matter.
- **Tension** — push-your-luck pull; "just one more" feel.
- **Variety** — distinctness from other events in its own decathlon.

## Output format (please follow exactly)

For each event:

    ### A1. 100 Metres
    Elegance: X | Agency: X | Tension: X | Variety: X
    [60–150 words: what works, what doesn't, failure modes observed, one suggested tweak if any]

After each decathlon's 10 events:

    - Standouts (top 2–3 events, why)
    - Weak events (bottom 2–3, why)
    - Cohesion as a 10-event set
    - One event you'd cut or redesign

At the very end:

    ### Head-to-head
    Which decathlon hangs together better as a whole, and why? Be direct.

    ### (Optional) Guess
    Your guess at which is the "original" and which is the tribute, if you want to call it.

# DECATHLON A

## A1. 100 Metres
Two sets of 4 dice. Throw set A, optionally reroll the whole set, then commit. Same for set B. Up to 5 rerolls total, shared across both sets. Score = sum of all 8 dice, but every **6** counts as **−6**. Max 40; min −48.

## A2. Long Jump
Best of 3 attempts. Two phases per attempt:
- **Run-up:** roll 5 dice. Each throw, freeze at least one die, reroll the rest. Frozen total must stay ≤ 8 or the jump fouls (0).
- **Jump:** take the dice you froze in run-up and throw them. Freeze at least one per throw, reroll the rest, until all frozen. High values are good.

Score = sum of the dice frozen in the jump.

## A3. Shot Put
Best of 3 attempts. Throw dice one at a time, up to 8 total. Stop any time and bank the sum. Any **1** invalidates the attempt (0).

## A4. High Jump
Roll 5 dice; if the sum ≥ current bar, you clear it. First bar at **10**, rising by **2** each clear. 3 attempts per bar; miss 3 and the event ends. Before your first attempt at a bar you may skip ahead; once you've attempted a bar, you're committed. Score = highest bar cleared (0 if you never clear the first you attempt). Busting still keeps your last cleared height.

## A5. 400 Metres
Four sets of 2 dice. Throw, optionally reroll, commit. Continue through all 4 sets. Up to 5 rerolls shared across the 4 sets. Score = sum of all 8 dice, with every **6** worth **−6**.

## A6. 110m Hurdles
Roll 5 dice. Reroll all 5 if you want, up to 5 pick-ups. Stop any time. Score = sum of all 5 dice.

## A7. Discus
Best of 3 attempts. Only **even** dice may be frozen. Roll 5 dice; freeze at least one even, then reroll the rest or stop. If a reroll shows zero evens, the attempt is invalid (0). Score = sum of frozen dice.

## A8. Pole Vault
Pick **1–8** dice to throw at the current bar. Bar cleared if sum ≥ bar **and** no die shows a **1**. First bar at **10**, rising by **2**. 3 attempts per bar; miss 3 and event ends. Skip allowed before first attempt at a bar; once attempted, committed. Score = highest bar cleared.

## A9. Javelin
Best of 3 attempts. Only **odd** dice may be frozen. Roll 6 dice; freeze at least one odd, then reroll the rest or stop. If a reroll shows zero odds, invalid (0). Score = sum of frozen dice.

## A10. 1500 Metres
Roll 8 dice, one at a time. After each die, keep it or reroll that single die. 5 rerolls total, pooled across all 8 dice. Score = sum of all 8, with every **6** worth **−6**.

# DECATHLON B

## B1. Pair Sums
Best of 3 rounds. Roll 10 dice. Pick a pair whose values sum to some target T; that T is fixed for the round. Lock any further pairs that also sum to T. Reroll unlocked dice for more pairs, or stop and bank. If a reroll can't form another pair summing to T, the round busts (0). Pair value by sum: 7 → 5 pts, 6/8 → 6, 5/9 → 7, 4/10 → 8, 3/11 → 9, 2/12 → 10.

## B2. Higher or Lower
Best of 3 rounds. Roll one die at a time, up to 10. Before each new roll call **Higher** or **Lower** than the last. Correct → add the new die's pips. Wrong → bust (0). Tie → no pips, no bust (die wasted). Stop any time to bank.

## B3. Sixes Multiplier
Best of 3 rounds. Pool of 10 dice rolled one at a time. Multiplier starts at ×1. **2–5:** pips × current multiplier add to score. **6:** 6 itself scores nothing, but multiplier increases by 1 for all future rolls. **1:** bust (0). Stop any time.

## B4. Stairs
Best of 3 rounds. Four stages: roll 1 die, then 2, then 3, then 4 (10 dice total). Every pip adds to your score — but a duplicate value within any single stage busts the round (0). Stop after any clean stage.

## B5. Salvo
Best of 3 rounds. Up to 3 throws per round, up to 4 dice each, 10 dice pool max. Any **1** in a throw busts the round (0). Clean throw adds all pips to the total. Stop after any clean throw.

## B6. 24
Best of 3 rounds. 5 tries. Each try: roll 2 dice, keep 1. Kept dice add up, except a kept **6** doesn't add pips — it instead multiplies your running total (a second kept 6 triples it, etc.). Hit exactly **24** → 40 pts. Under 24 → lose 4 pts per pip short. Each kept 6 also costs 6 pts. Over 24 → 0.

## B7. Double Down
Best of 3 rounds. Roll 10 dice as 5 sequential pairs. After each pair, lock it or reroll the whole pair; you have 10 rerolls total for the round. Pair scoring: normally the sum. A single **6** scores its own pips *and* doubles the other die (6+4 = 6 + 8 = 14; 6+5 = 6 + 10 = 16). Two **6s** double each other → **24**. A single **1** busts the pair to **0** (but may be rerolled). **Snake eyes** (both 1s) bust the entire round and cannot be rerolled. Round ends when 5 pairs are locked or snake eyes bust it.

## B8. Ascent
Best of 3 rounds. Turn 1: roll 1 die, its value is the sum to beat. Each later turn you must roll strictly more dice than last turn (up to 10 total) and the new sum must exceed the previous sum. Sum > previous → cleared turn. Sum = previous → no progress, no bust, but dice count still advances. Sum < previous → round busts (0). Stop any time. Score = n(n+1)/2 where n is cleared turns (1 → 1 pt, 2 → 3, …, 10 → 55).

## B9. Roundup
Best of 3 rounds. 10-die pool; roll 6 to start. Pick any value that appears in the roll and lock every die showing it — this is your **committed value** for the round. Then stop & bank, or press: roll up to 6 of the remaining dice (fewer if fewer remain) and lock any that match your committed value. Continue pressing or stop after each press. If a press yields zero matches, the round busts (0). Scoring: sum of pips on locked dice + (size of set)². Four 5s → 20 + 16 = 36. Five 1s → 5 + 25 = 30.

## B10. Snakes
Best of 3 rounds. Up to 10 dice over up to 5 rolls. Each roll, choose how many dice to throw (1 up to dice remaining). Pips 2–6 add to the total. Every **1** is a "snake" worth **−15**. Stop any time. A negative banked total scores 0.
